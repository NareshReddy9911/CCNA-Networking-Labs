DR/BDR Multicast Verification

Wireshark was used to observe OSPF traffic on the broadcast network.

Observed OSPF multicast addresses:

224.0.0.5
All OSPF routers

224.0.0.6
All OSPF designated routers

During the test, OSPF LS Update traffic was observed from the routers.

The capture shows traffic sent to 224.0.0.6 and OSPF traffic distributed through 224.0.0.5.

This confirms the use of OSPF multicast communication for DR/BDR operation.
